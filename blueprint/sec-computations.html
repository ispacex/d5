Proposition 5.1 Computer-assisted
#

In each of the following groups \(D_n(G)=\gamma _n(G)\) for all \(n\):

  1. all \(2\)-groups of order \(8,16,32,64,128\) (\(2665\) groups) and all groups of order \(256\) of class \(\ge 3\) (\(24\, 328\) groups);

  2. all \(3\)-groups of order \(81\), \(243\), \(729\) (\(586\) groups);

  3. all \(1202\) groups of order \(3^7\) of class \(4\) that pass the sieve, by two methods with identical results (\(1202\) groups, \(6010\) levels, no discrepancies): the lattice of §5 in its C implementation and the truncated Magnus algebra of §7, item ?? (code and description: https://gitlab.com/ispacex/cflint-core, docs/analysis.md, §6.1); the numpy code of compute/ was run on a sample of \(49\) of them (every \(25\)th), with the same answer;

  4. all \(15\) groups of order \(625\) (a control).

Every \(3\)-group of class \(4\) and order \(3^5\), \(3^6\), \(3^7\) (\(6\), \(71\), \(1309\) groups) is metabelian (this is proved in Proposition 6.5, also in Lean). Moreover, \(D_5(Q)=1\) for every metabelian group \(Q\) of order \(512\) and class \(3\): the sieve leaves \(2792\) of the \(1\, 587\, 389\) such groups (out of \(1\, 708\, 439\) groups of order \(512\) and \(p\)-class \(\ge 3\)), and for each of the \(2792\) the lattice computation gives \(D_4(Q)=D_5(Q)=1\).

Theorem 5.4 Computer-assisted
#

  1. \(D_5(G)\subseteq \gamma _4(G)\) for every finite group \(G\) whose Sylow \(2\)-subgroup has order at most \(2^{10}\); in particular for every group of order less than \(2048\).

  2. \(D_n(G)=\gamma _n(G)\) for all \(n\) and every finite group \(G\) of order less than \(512\).

  3. \(D_5(G)/\gamma _5(G)\) has no \(3\)-torsion for every finite group \(G\) whose Sylow \(3\)-subgroup has order at most \(3^7\); in fact \(D_5(Q)=\gamma _5(Q)\) for every \(3\)-group \(Q\) of order at most \(3^7\). (Extended to \(3^9\) in Theorem 6.16.)

Remark 5.5 pruning the generation tree
#

Let \(Q\) be a finite \(2\)-group of class \(3\) with cyclic centre, \(d=d(Q)\), \(C=C_Q(\gamma _2(Q))\), \(X=\gamma _2(Q)/(\gamma _2(Q)\cap Z(Q))\). The commutator map \(X\times Q/C\to \gamma _3(Q)\) is a non-degenerate bilinear pairing into the cyclic group \(\gamma _3(Q)\le Z(Q)\); hence \(X\cong Q/C\), a quotient of \(Q_{ab}\), and \(X\) has rank \(\le \min (d,d(d-1)/2)\). If \(|\gamma _3(Q)|=2^e\), then \(Q_{ab}\) has two invariants \(\ge 2^e\) (the map \((u,v)\mapsto [u,v,z]\) factors through \(\Lambda ^2Q_{ab}\)) and \(X\) has an element of order \(\ge 2^e\). For a minimal counterexample \(Q\) (Lemma 5.2) with central involution \(n\) this gives conditions on every quotient \(V=Q/N\) with \(N\le \Phi (Q)\) (with \(n\in N\le \Phi (Q)\) for the second), which pass to further quotients and so may be used to cut whole subtrees of the \(p\)-group generation tree: (i) there is a cyclic \(K\), \(\gamma _3(V)\le K\le \gamma _2(V)\cap Z(V)\), with \(\mathrm{rk}(\gamma _2(V)/K)\le \min (d,d(d-1)/2)\); (ii) for such a \(K\), \(|Q|\ge 2|K|\, |A\vee B\vee E_1|\, |A\vee V_{ab}\vee E_2|\), where \(A=\gamma _2(V)/K\), \(B=V/C_V(\gamma _2(V))\), \(E_1=\mathbb {Z}/2^{e_V+1}\), \(E_2=E_1^2\), \(2^{e_V}=|\gamma _3(V)|\), and \(\vee \) is the componentwise maximum of abelian invariants. In particular every such \(V\) of class \(3\) has \(d+4e_V+2\le \log _2|Q|\). These conditions are proved by hand; the proofs are in compute/pq2048/README.md. Condition (i) is vacuous for \(d\le 3\) and below order \(2^{2d+1}\); condition (ii) is the one that cuts.

Proof ▶

See the paper. By hand; not formalized.
