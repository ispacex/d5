The Kourovka Notebook: who solved what, and how

Every problem of the Kourovka Notebook (unsolved problems in group theory, eds. E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov), with its statement, its status and who solved it: people, or people with AI. Modelled on T. Tao's “AI contributions to Erdős problems”. Statements, answers and marks come from the Notebook's source (arXiv:1401.0300v50): since 2026 it marks a solution proposed by AI with ♯ until a mathematician confirms it, and with “AI solution confirmed by …” once checked. Beyond the Notebook: our notes, each with a primary source: solutions not yet in the Notebook, and which AI systems are behind the solutions. Last checked: 2026-11-26; updated weekly.

Who is behind the AI solutions

  • M. Kinyon, J. Urban: OpenAI GPT-6 Astra via Codex + GAP; the work was then reviewed by Claude Opus in Claude Code. A 48-hour experiment, 10–12 Sep 2026, 45 proposed answers. (kourovka-mathematics.pdf; github.com/JUrban/kour1)
  • OpenAI: An internal, unreleased OpenAI model; the PDFs do not name it. Only the Notebook ties the proofs to problem numbers. (ten-proofs-oai.pdf; Sharing AI progress in mathematics (6 Oct 2026))
  • P. Monticone (Harmonic): Aristotle (Harmonic): solutions found and verified in Lean 4 autonomously. (arXiv:2607.17477; github.com/pitmonticone/Kourovka)
  • Achyuth Jayadevan: The system behind the mathematics is not named; the Lean formalization used GPT-Astra (or a fine-tuned DeepSeek V4.1) through a custom LangGraph harness. About 25 Zenodo records, Sep 2026. (arXiv:2609.10281; kourovka-21-40-paper.pdf)
  • Ionin, Semidetnov: Codex with ChatGPT 5.6 Sol; Claude Desktop with Opus 5. (arXiv:2608.29219)
  • T. Gong, M. R. Zeng, Y. Yang: Albilich, the authors' own system (LLM + computer algebra). (arXiv:2607.27705)
  • Nilradical: The nilradical.ai agent with Lean formalization; a human final-stage approver (A. Rizzoli for 21.35). In their words, “an agent-written mathematical account, not a refereed article”. (nilradical-kourovka-21-35.pdf)
  • A. Ferudun (Mercury Software): Unnamed “AI-assisted tools”. (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23445980)
  • R. Sater: Unnamed generative AI. (arXiv:2608.12432)
  • G. Cat, B. G. Di Siena, J. DeCaro, J. Liao: The Notebook labels their solutions as AI solutions; the texts do not name the system. (kourovka_15-26-ai.pdf; arXiv:2609.17027)

How it is kept

Weekly: the latest source of the Notebook is fetched from arXiv and parsed again (site/kourovka.py), changes of status go into the log below; then a search of arXiv, the Notebook's site, blogs and MathOverflow with the queries below. A note is added only after its primary source has been read. Know of a solution that is missing? Open an issue. The data: site/kourovka.json, site/kourovka-problems.json. The statements are converted from the Notebook's LaTeX automatically; where they differ, the PDF on arXiv is right.
