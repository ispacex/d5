Result beyond the paper 1 \(D_5=1\) for \(Q_{ab}\cong C_3\times C_9\times C_9\)
✓

Let \(Q\) be a finite group of class \(\le 4\) with \(Q/\gamma _2(Q)\cong C_3\times C_9\times C_9\). Then \(D_5(Q)=1\). No condition on \(\gamma _4(Q)\) is assumed.

Lean: GroupDimension.D_five_eq_bot_of_class_four_ab399, GroupDimension.Ab399.D_five_eq_bot_of_class_four_ab399, GroupDimension.Star.two_smul_gen_eq.

Not in the paper (the authors’ decision): the proof on paper is MATH-446 (wiki 891 §10, one review per 703), the Lean proof group-dimension#48 (MATH-448). It covers γ_4 of exponent 9 (e.g. γ_4 ≅ C_9), the case that Remark 6.13 leaves open, for this type of Q_ab; for γ_4^3 = 1 it is a case of Theorem 6.11. Star.two_smul_gen_eq is the exact identity (⋆) of the proof of Theorem 6.11, for Tahara’s data and any admissible τ in a group of class ≤ 4, with no condition on γ_4; the Lean proof of Theorem 6.11 does not use it. GroupDimension.Ab399.D_five_eq_bot_of_class_four_ab399 takes Tahara’s inclusion as a hypothesis, the unprefixed one discharges it. Only the finite form of class ≤ 4 is in Lean; the general form D_5(G) = γ_5(G) for G_ab ≅ C_3 × C_9 × C_9 is not formalized. Types with at most two invariants ≥ 9 are covered by own:thm126 (MATH-468); types with three invariants ≥ 9 are open (MATH-450).

Result beyond the paper 2 \(D_5\subseteq \Omega _{\mu -1}\): the third invariant of \(Q_{ab}\)
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • GroupDimension.D_five_pow_eq_one_of_third_invariant
  • GroupDimension.D_five_eq_bot_of_two_large_invariants

Let \(Q\) be a finite \(3\)-group of class \(\le 4\) and \(\mu \ge 0\) such that at most two invariants of \(Q/\gamma _2(Q)\) exceed \(3^\mu \) (e.g. \(3^\mu \) the third largest invariant). Then \(z^{3^{\mu -1}}=1\) for every \(z\in D_5(Q)\) (with \(3^{\mu -1}:=1\) for \(\mu =0\)). In particular, if at most two invariants of \(Q/\gamma _2(Q)\) are \(\ge 9\), then \(D_5(Q)=1\), with no condition on \(\gamma _4(Q)\).

Lean: GroupDimension.D_five_pow_eq_one_of_third_invariant, GroupDimension.D_five_eq_bot_of_two_large_invariants.

Not in the paper: the proof on paper is Theorem 12.6 of wiki 891 §12.3 (MATH-451, reviewed per 703) with its case μ ≤ 1, Theorem 11.2 (§11.2, MATH-450); the Lean proof is group-dimension#54 (MATH-468). It evaluates the exact identity (⋆) (Star.two_smul_gen_eq, node own:ab399) term by term after multiplying by 3^{μ-1}. In Lean the hypothesis is stated intrinsically: the 3^μ-th powers of Q_ab contain at most 9 elements of order dividing 3. The conclusion is the exponent bound on D_5(Q); together with D_5(Q) ⊆ γ_4(Q)^3 (Corollary 6.12) it gives D_5(Q) ⊆ Ω_{μ-1}(γ_4(Q)), but that combination is not stated in Lean. The case μ = 1 contains the type [3,9,9] of own:ab399. Only the finite form of class ≤ 4 is in Lean; the general form D_5(G) = γ_5(G) is not formalized. Types with three invariants ≥ 9 (μ ≥ 2) are open.
