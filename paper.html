Abstract

For a group $G$ let $D_n(G)=G\cap(1+\D(G)^n)$ be its integral dimension subgroups and $\gamma_n(G)$ its lower central series. Whether $D_5(G)\subseteq\gamma_4(G)$ for every group is open (Kourovka Notebook, Problem 20.84). For Lie rings the analogous inclusion was recently proved by Ionin, Mikhailov and Petrov, and formally verified in Lean. This note collects what is known, records an elementary reduction of the group question to finite metabelian $2$-groups of class three, analyses which steps of the Lie-ring proof have no group-theoretic counterpart, and reports computational evidence: a computer search shows that a counterexample among finite $2$-groups of class three has order at least $2^{12}$. For $3$-torsion in $D_5/\gamma_5$ we prove $D_5(G)=\gamma_5(G)$ whenever $G/\gamma_2(G)$ is finite and has at most one invariant larger than $3$, and, with a computer search, for all $3$-groups of order at most $3^9$. We do not solve the problem.
