D Gupta–Tahara at \(p=3\) through Tahara’s Theorem 6.1

Let \(p\) be an odd prime, \(G/\gamma _2(G)\) a finite \(p\)-group and \([\gamma _2(G),\gamma _2(G)]\subseteq \gamma _5(G)\) (e.g. \(G\) metabelian). Then \(D_5(G)=\gamma _5(G)\). In particular \(D_5=\gamma _5\) for finite metabelian \(3\)-groups, which is [GT85, Thm. 3.2(c)] at \(p=3\).
